As of Q2 2026, all you need is 48GB of RAM to do heavy work + frontier-level local LLM that's very fast (69 tokens/sec is an incredible rate).

Install [pi agent harness](https://pi.dev/), then buy $5 bucks of OpenRouter credits, inside pi run `/login` to configure your OpenRouter key, then pick GLM 5.2 or DeepSeek V4 Flash, then ask pi to help you download [Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q5_K_XL](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTP-GGUF?show_file_info=Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q5_K_XL.gguf) from HuggingFace (Google it yourself first and understand why HuggingFace is a trusted repository). 

Why pi, you may ask? I don't really like heros. But yea, [Mario Zechner](https://mariozechner.at/) is my new hero.

**Qwen3.6 35B A3B** is a marvel of an invention. It's a mixture of experts model and is much faster over its cousin, the dense model Qwen3.6 27B. 

The Q5 means 5-bit quant, which you don't have to know what it means, but it's better than 4-bit quant, which is already ideal and what experts run.

Once pi downloads the model files, tell it to add the model to pi's own `models.json`. Then tell pi to install [llama.cpp via homebrew](https://formulae.brew.sh/formula/llama.cpp) and tell it to teach you how to start/stop the llama.cpp server from the command line.

You're ready to swap out OpenRouter for your local Qwen3.6 instance. Type `/model` + press enter. Say hi to it, test basic operations and **work with pi to fix/improve** based on your needs.

**Stay away from pi extensions**. If you're not scared, learn about **info stealers** ([1](https://youtu.be/lSa_wHW1pgQ?si=x_lr_3YIMEdYilXC), [2](https://youtu.be/e7SqbgQhQV8?si=JZmvj9Hg_7-tBX2G)). For example, don't install web search pi extensions. The reality is you don't really need it. If your model needs to search the internet, instead of doing that, tell it to download data from places that you trust (otherwise you shouldn't download it, right?) to your `/tmp` directory (literally tell pi this). Have a conversation with pi about what you pulled from the internet like that. Reason being, unsanitized incoming data is dangerous and it's how you can get hacked. **Don't get hacked**. To give example, I have personally seen my agent (using other setups and tools) visit strange websites that have nothing to do with what I asked it to do or search the web for. You risk being targeted with a prompt injection. If you really need your local agent to research the web, isolate pi (not the llm server) inside a Docker VM. But skip that, it's for losers. Good luck and have fun with your new Macbook Pro M5 Pro. It's an amazing machine.
